01 / ABOUT ME

Technology is most powerful when it solves real problems.

I bring together a security mindset, a developer’s curiosity, and a teacher’s patience.

I’m a cybersecurity graduate and technology educator with practical interests across software development, data analysis, front-end web development, on-chain analysis, and AI-powered automation.

My approach starts with understanding the problem, breaking it into manageable parts, and building a solution that is useful, secure, and easy to maintain. I also enjoy teaching young learners and beginners because explaining a concept clearly is one of the best ways to understand it deeply.

I’m especially interested in the connection between cybersecurity, privacy, digital trust, and responsible technology. That perspective shapes how I think about systems, users, and the data they depend on.
